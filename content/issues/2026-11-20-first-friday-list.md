## A practical AI tip for this week

If someone in the family is heading to an open day or preparing an apprenticeship application, try using a chatbot to **help you plan**, not to write the work for you.

You could ask it to:

- turn a revision list into a simple week-by-week plan, or
- suggest questions to ask at Saturday’s college or university open day, or in an apprenticeship interview.

Then put the plan into your own words, and practise the answers yourself.

For assessed school or college work, the Joint Council for Qualifications is clear: **the work you submit must be your own**. You must not copy wording, ideas or outputs from the internet or from an AI tool and claim them as yours. If a teacher has said AI is allowed for a particular task, JCQ says you must name the tool and the date, keep a copy of your prompts and the output (for example a screenshot), and briefly explain how you used it. If you are unsure, ask the teacher before you start. [JCQ information for candidates, effective from 1 September 2026](https://www.jcq.org.uk/knowledge-hub/non-examination-assessments/)
